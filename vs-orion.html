A fan moves air around your room outside your bed. The Smart Topper circulates perfectly calibrated active airflow directly against your skin from head to toe through your fitted sheet. The cooling source is ambient air coming into contact with your body and bedding, absorbing large amounts of heat, then being pushed out of the sleep environment, taking the heat with it. You and your bed are left cool and dry.
When your body detects intense, localized cold against the skin, it narrows the blood vessels near that spot to protect your core temperature. This is called vasoconstriction, and it's a built-in defense response. The side effect: it reduces blood flow and heat transfer right at the point where a cold water pad is working hardest. Your body is actively fighting the cooling system meant to help it.

Gentle, distributed airflow doesn't trigger the same response. There's no single cold spot for your body to defend against, so it doesn't compensate. Cooling stays consistent through the night rather than your body adapting to resist it.

There's also a second problem water cooling can't solve: your body releases moisture continuously while you sleep, creating a warm, humid layer of air right against your skin. A cold water surface can chill the mattress below that layer, but it does nothing to clear the trapped, humid air at skin level. Moving air clears that microclimate entirely and enables evaporative cooling. It solves two problems at once.
