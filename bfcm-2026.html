Yes. The Smart Topper has dual zones for personalized temperature control, with a range of 20 levels that adjust for both heating and cooling. A fan moves air around your room outside your bed. The Smart Topper circulates perfectly calibrated airflow directly against your skin from head to toe through your fitted sheet — absorbing heat and pushing it out of your sleep environment, taking your body heat with it. You and your bed are left cool and dry.
When your body detects intense, localized cold against the skin, it narrows blood vessels near that spot to protect your core temperature. This is called vasoconstriction — a built-in defense response. The side effect: it reduces blood flow and heat transfer right where the cold water pad is working hardest. Your body is actively fighting the cooling system meant to help it.

Gentle, distributed airflow doesn't trigger that response. Cooling stays consistent all night instead of your body adapting to resist it.

Air also solves a second problem water can't: your body releases moisture continuously while you sleep, creating a warm, humid microclimate right against your skin. Moving air clears that entirely and enables evaporative cooling — the same mechanism your body already uses when it sweats.
