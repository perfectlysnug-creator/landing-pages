The problem with water cooling

Here's what nobody tells you
about water cooling.

When you buy Eight Sleep, ChiliPad, or Orion, here is what happens: a machine on your floor chills a tank of treated water. That water runs through tubes inside a pad on your mattress. You sleep on top of it.

The cold has to travel through the pad and sheets to reach your body, and cooling temperature adjustments have a response time of up to 30 minutes. Meanwhile the system traps humidity under your covers: a damp environment that can feel sticky, clammy and uncomfortable.

Why air is different

Air cooling works differently.
Here's why it matters.

The Smart Topper sends perfectly calibrated airflow directly against your skin: through your fitted sheet from head to toe, right where heat builds up most. When this active airflow reaches your skin and sleep microclimate it absorbs heat and then gets pushed out of your bed, taking your excess body heat with it. This is the primary cooling mechanism, removing up to 122W of body heat per night – more than the human body can produce. In addition to this cooling mechanism our sleepers benefit from evaporative cooling and a windchill effect that circulates around you (a fan limit can be set to ensure it's not too much!). Your body loses heat faster than it would lying on a cold surface slowly warming back up.

In contact with your skin
Water's cooling effect has to travel through a waterproof pad and your sheets before it can reach you. Air flows directly against your skin through your sheets — nothing in between. That's a fundamentally shorter path from source to body.
Removes heat instead of chilling a surface
Cold water sits underneath you and waits. Moving air absorbs heat from your body and carries it out of the sleep environment. Evaporative cooling and windchill provide additional cooling benefits. Your body loses heat faster than it would on a cold surface slowly warming back up.
No trapped humidity
Water-based systems build up moisture under your covers as you sleep. Air carries humidity away as it flows. You stay cool and dry through the night instead of waking up damp.

Hot flash + night sweats

When a hot flash hits at 2 AM, you need cooling in seconds. Not minutes.

Water cooling is already working at a fixed temperature. It can't respond to a sudden spike. Air cooling adjusts the moment your body temperature changes.
