The problem with water cooling

Here's what nobody tells you
about water cooling.

When you buy Eight Sleep, ChiliPad, or Ooler, here is what actually happens. A machine on your floor chills a tank of water. That water runs through tubes inside a pad on your mattress. You sleep on top of it.

The cold has to travel through the pad, through your sheets, and hope it reaches your body. Meanwhile the system traps humidity under your covers: a warm, damp environment that works against you.

Why air is different

Air cooling works differently.
Here's why it matters.

The Smart Topper uses air: not air blowing at your face, but air that flows directly against your skin, under your sheets, right where the heat is building up. When warm air absorbs heat from your body and leaves the sleep environment, two things happen: evaporative cooling and a windchill effect. Your body loses heat faster than it would lying on a cold surface slowly warming back up.

Not just cooling

Most people find the Smart Topper because they sleep hot. Many end up using the heating just as much.

It does not blast heat at you. It works more like a heated car seat: gentle, even warmth that radiates from the air surrounding your body. Comfortable enough to fall asleep in. Consistent enough to hold through the night. Twenty levels. Thermostat-like adjustments throughout the night. The same technology that pulls heat away in July works in reverse in January.

The mechanism

How the Smart Topper removes heat from your body.

The topper has a breathable surface layer. Temperature-controlled air flows through it and directly against your skin. That air absorbs heat from your body and carries it away as it leaves the sleep environment. The system reads your temperature throughout the night and keeps adjusting.
